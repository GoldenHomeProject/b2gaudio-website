How to listen to your email while driving

Turn dead windshield time into inbox time — safely, with your eyes on the road.

The short version: install a hands-free email app that works with CarPlay. DriveMail Voice connects to your Gmail, Outlook, and iCloud accounts and reads your unread email aloud the moment you plug into CarPlay — then lets you reply or forward by voice.

Download DriveMail Voice — free

Set it up in three steps

1

Add your accounts

Connect Gmail, Outlook, or iCloud once. Everything merges into a single unread stream.

2

Connect CarPlay

Plug in or connect wirelessly. DriveMail starts reading your unread email. Oldest or newest first is your choice in Settings.

3

Listen and reply

Pause, skip, replay, or say a reply. Mark messages read or leave them for later.

Why listening beats glancing

A five-second glance at 70 mph covers the length of a football field with your eyes off the road. Having email read aloud — with voice replies — means you stay caught up without ever picking up your phone.

Questions people ask

How can I listen to my email while driving?

Use a hands-free email app like DriveMail Voice. It connects to Gmail, Outlook, and iCloud and reads your unread email aloud on Apple CarPlay, so you never look at your phone.

Is it safe to check email while driving?

Reading email on your phone screen while driving is dangerous and illegal in many places. Listening to email read aloud, with voice replies, keeps your eyes on the road and your hands on the wheel.

Can I reply to email by voice while driving?

Yes. DriveMail Voice lets you dictate a reply or forward, confirm, and send entirely by voice through CarPlay.
