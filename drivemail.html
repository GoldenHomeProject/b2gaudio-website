DriveMail Voice

Your emails, read aloud.

The hands-free email app for drivers. DriveMail Voice reads your unread emails aloud, lets you reply by voice, and works seamlessly with Apple CarPlay. Stay informed without taking your eyes off the road.

Download on the App Store

Everything You Need, Hands-Free

🔊

Read Aloud

DriveMail Voice reads your unread emails aloud — sender, subject, and body. Listen to your inbox like a podcast while you drive.

🚗

CarPlay Support

Seamless Apple CarPlay integration. Control playback, reply, skip, and mark as read — all from your car's built-in display.

🎤

Voice Replies

Reply to emails with your voice. Dictate your response, hear it read back, and confirm before sending. No typing needed.

✨

AI Summaries

Get quick AI-powered summaries of long emails. Decide what needs your attention now and what can wait — all processed on-device.

🧹

Smart Reading

Automatically skips signatures, legal disclaimers, tracking links, and boilerplate. You only hear what actually matters.

🔒

Privacy First

All processing happens on your device. No backend servers — your mail goes straight from your provider to your phone, and anything cached there is encrypted at rest.

How It Works

1

Connect Your Email

Connect Gmail, Outlook, or iCloud. Outlook uses secure OAuth sign-in; Gmail and iCloud use app-specific passwords. Your credentials stay in your device's keychain — never on our servers.

2

Start Listening

Tap play or connect to CarPlay. DriveMail Voice fetches your unread emails and starts reading them aloud, one by one.

3

Take Action

After each email, choose to reply by voice, mark as read, get an AI summary, or skip to the next one. All hands-free.

4

Drive Safe

Stay on top of your inbox without ever touching your phone. Adjust speed, pause, replay — all with simple taps or voice commands.
