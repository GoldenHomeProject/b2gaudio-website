Mandemic

Support

Mandemic for iPhone and Android (coming soon)

Email b2gaudio@gmail.com and we will get back to you. If you are ever in danger, call 911 first. Mandemic isn't out yet; this page covers how the app works at launch.

The basics

Is this a dating app? No. Mandemic is strictly platonic. It's for guys who want friends to play pickleball, golf, see a show, or take a trip with. Using it to hook up gets an account banned.

Does it cost anything? No. The app is free. When an activity itself costs money, like court time or tickets, the group splits it between themselves.

Which phones? iPhone and Android.

Where is it available? It launches in one city first, so there are enough guys nearby on day one. Other cities follow.

Signing up

How do I sign up? Enter your email and we'll send you a 6-digit code, or use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. There's no password to remember.

My code didn't arrive. Wait a minute, check your spam or junk folder, make sure the address is right, and tap "Send again." Temporary or throwaway email addresses can't be used.

Is my email shown to anyone? No. Your email is never shown to other members.

Why do you ask my birth year? You must be 18 to join, and plans centered on alcohol are 21 and older.

Do I have to answer the faith and politics questions? No. That screen is optional and skippable. If you answer, it's only used for matching when you say it matters to you, and it stays hidden from your profile unless you choose to show it.

Plans and crews

How do I join a plan? Tap "In." Spots are first come, first served. If you can't make it after all, tap "Not this time" as early as you can.

Can I post my own plan? Yes. Pick the activity, time, place, number of spots and who can see it: your crew, a tribe, or anyone nearby. New accounts can post plans after their first check-in at a meetup.

Can I bring my kids? Every plan is marked either "kids welcome" or "guys only."

Do I have to organize plans? No. The app suggests your crew's next outing every week or two. Anyone can tweak it.

How do I leave a crew? Open the crew and tap "Leave crew." There's no penalty. At the end of each 8-week season you can also privately choose whether to keep rolling with the same guys.

Your reliability score

How does it work? It goes up when you show up and check in, and when you pay your share on time. Cancelling more than 48 hours ahead has no effect. Cancelling 24 to 48 hours ahead is a small hit, under 24 hours is a bigger one, and a no-show is the biggest. New members show "New" until their third meetup.

Something came up. Everyone gets one "life happens" pass a month. Use it when you cancel and that cancellation won't count.

My score is wrong. Tap the event in your score history and choose "Dispute." Check-in data decides attendance. If there's no proof either way, your score doesn't change. Still stuck? Email us with the plan name and date.

Money

How does splitting work? The organizer pays the venue, the app works out each person's share, and you pay the organizer with Venmo, Cash App or PayPal through a link in the app. The organizer marks you paid.

Someone hasn't paid me back. Mandemic never holds or moves money, so we can't collect it for you. Late or missing payments do count against that person's reliability score.

I paid but I'm still marked unpaid. Ask the organizer in the plan chat first. If that doesn't fix it, dispute it from your score history.

Safety

How do I report or block someone? Tap the three dots on their profile or on any message, then "Report" or "Block." Reports are reviewed by a person. Blocking hides you from each other.

Tips for meeting new guys: meet in a group at a public place first, get yourself there and home, and tell someone where you're going. Your home address never needs to be on your profile; share a pickup spot only in a private chat, and only if you want to.

Your account

How do I change my email? Settings, then Account, then Email. You'll confirm the new address with a code.

How do I delete my account? Settings, then Account, then "Delete account." Your profile and data are removed as described in the Privacy Policy.

Can I get a copy of my data? Yes. Email us from the address on your account.
