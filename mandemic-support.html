Mandemic isn't out yet. Questions, ideas, or want to help shape it? Email b2gaudio@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

Is this a dating app? No. Mandemic is strictly platonic. It's for guys who want friends to play pickleball, golf, see a show, or take a trip with. Using it to hook up gets an account banned.

Does it cost anything? The app is planned to be free. When an activity itself costs money, like court time or tickets, the group splits it between themselves.

Which phones? iPhone and Android.

Where will it launch? In one city first, so there are enough guys nearby on day one. Other cities follow.

Do I have to organize plans? No. You can post your own plans anytime, but the app also suggests your crew's next outing for you.

Can I bring my kids? Every plan is marked either "kids welcome" or "guys only," so you can choose.
