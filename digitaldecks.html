Digital Decks

Scan it. Collect it. Build a real deck.

Point your camera at a Pokémon card and Digital Decks tells you what it is, how rare it is, and roughly what it's worth. Keep a digital collection, then build real decks from the cards you actually own. Made for kids — no ads, no accounts, nothing leaves the device.

Download on the App Store

One-time purchase. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

Built for how kids actually play

Big buttons, read-aloud, and a deck builder that only ever uses cards they really own.

📸

Scan any card

Point the camera at the collector number and Digital Decks identifies the card instantly. Recognition runs on the device — no photo is ever saved or uploaded.

⭐

See how rare it is

Plain-language rarity with approximate pull odds — "about 1 in 13 packs" — so a seven-year-old understands why a card is special. Rare pulls get a celebration.

💰

Rough value

Ungraded market price plus typical PSA 10 and CGC 10 listing prices, clearly labelled as estimates — never presented as an appraisal.

🗂

Digital collection

Track every card and how many copies. Filter by type, set or rarity, and see what the whole collection is roughly worth.

🛠

Real deck building

Pick one or two types and Digital Decks assembles the best legal deck it can from cards you own — complete evolution lines, trainers, energy — and rates it out of five stars.

🗣

Reads aloud

Tap the speaker and Digital Decks reads card details and coaching tips out loud, so kids who can't read yet can still use it on their own.

🧒

A profile per kid

Two children, one iPad, two completely separate collections and deck lists. Neither can change the other's cards.

🔒

Safe by design

No accounts, no chat, no ads, no tracking. The single link out of the app sits behind a parental gate.

How it works

From a shoebox of cards to a deck worth playing.

1

Make a player

Each kid picks a name, a picture and a colour. Everything they collect stays under their own profile.

2

Scan a card

Point at the number in the corner — like 4/102. Digital Decks finds the card, shows the artwork, the set, the rarity and the price. Tap to add it to the collection.

3

Build a deck

Choose one or two types. Digital Decks builds the strongest legal deck it can from the cards that kid owns, keeping evolution lines together and matching energy to attack costs.

4

Learn what's missing

Every deck comes with a star rating, kid-friendly coaching, and a "Make It Better" list — the exact cards that would improve it. Read aloud on request.

A kids' app that collects nothing

Not "we protect your data" — there simply is no data. Everything stays on the device.

About prices. Digital Decks shows rough estimates gathered from public market data and current listings. Graded figures reflect what sellers are asking, not what cards have sold for, and nothing accounts for condition, centring or authenticity. Digital Decks does not provide appraisals or financial advice — please don't use it to decide what to pay for, sell, or insure a card.
