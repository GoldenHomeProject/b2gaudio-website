Mandemic

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version: we collect what it takes to match you with guys nearby and keep meetups reliable and safe. Your location is approximate, your home address is never asked for, faith and politics are optional and private, and we never ask about race or ethnicity. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and deleting your account deletes your data.

This policy covers the Mandemic app for iPhone and Android ("the App"), provided by B2Gaudio ("we," "our"). Mandemic has not launched yet; this policy describes how the App will handle your information when it does. Until then, the only information we receive is what you choose to email us, which we use only to reply.

1. What we collect

When you sign up

  • Your email address, confirmed with a code, or the sign-in details Apple or Google share with us if you use Sign in with Apple or Google (Apple lets you hide your real email). It is never shown to other members.
  • Your first name and birth year.
  • The activities you're into and the times you're usually free.
  • Whether you have kids and their rough age range (for example "school-age"). We never ask for children's names or details.
  • Your answers about drinking and how competitive you like things.
  • Optional: a photo and a short bio.

Optional, sensitive information. If you choose to answer the "What matters to you" screen, we collect your faith and political leaning and how much each matters to you. We ask for your clear permission first, use it only for matching, keep it hidden from your profile unless you choose to show it, and you can remove it at any time.

Location. With your permission, the App uses your approximate location (your general area, not a street address) to measure distance to plans. When you check in at a meetup, the App confirms you are at the venue; we keep a record of the check-in (the plan and the time), not a history of where you go. You can turn location off and set your area by hand.

What you do in the App. Plans you post or join, your crews, chat messages, check-ins, cancellations, your reliability score history, whether you've been marked paid for a shared cost, and reports or blocks you make.

Your device. A notification token so we can send reminders, the result of Apple's or Google's check that the App is running on a real device (used to keep bots out), and crash reports so we can fix bugs.

What we do not collect: your race or ethnicity, your home address, your contacts, payment card or bank details, or your activity in other apps and websites.

2. How we use it

  • To run the App: your profile, plans, crews, chat, reminders and calendar invites.
  • To match you with plans and crews, using activities, distance, schedules and, only if you say they matter, your optional answers.
  • To calculate your reliability score and settle disputes about it.
  • To keep the community safe: preventing fake accounts, reviewing reports, and enforcing the Terms of Service.
  • To fix problems and improve the App.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. What other members see

  • Your first name, activities, reliability score and how many guys you've met in person.
  • Your photo, only once you share a plan or a crew with someone.
  • Distance to a plan's venue, never your own location.
  • Your messages, only to the members of that chat.
  • Your plans, only to the audience you choose: your crew, a tribe, or anyone nearby.
  • Faith and politics answers, only if you choose to show them.

Your email address, birth year, and the details of your score history are never shown to other members.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the App: cloud hosting and database storage, email delivery for sign-in codes, Apple and Google for sign-in, notifications and device checks, crash reporting, and map and public-event listing services. Map and event services receive a search area, not your identity. These providers may use your information only to provide their service to us.

When you tap a Venmo, Cash App or PayPal link to pay a share, you leave the App and that company's privacy policy applies. We receive no payment information from them.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith it is needed to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. Your reliability score only uses your recent history, and older records are deleted over time. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and personal information within 30 days. We may keep a minimal record (such as a scrambled form of the email address) of an account banned for breaking the rules, so it can't simply sign up again, and anything the law requires us to keep.

6. Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and stored with access limited to what's needed to run the App. No system is perfectly secure, so if you ever believe your account has been compromised, email us right away.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and answers at any time in the App.
  • Remove your optional faith and politics answers at any time, which also stops them being used for matching.
  • Turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account in the App (Settings, then Account).
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights over your information. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever they live, and won't treat you differently for making one.

8. Adults only

Mandemic is for adults 18 and older. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

9. Where your data is stored

Mandemic is operated from the United States, and your information is stored in the United States.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the current version is always published here. We'll tell you in the App before a material change takes effect.

11. Contact
