B2Gaudio

Music is. Better 2Gether.

Share music and listen together with friends over WiFi. One phone hosts, everyone listens in sync. No accounts needed. The phones connect to each other directly, so your own music files play without internet.

Download on the App Store

Everything You Need to Listen Together

📶

WiFi-Only Sharing

Phones connect to each other directly, over your WiFi network or Apple's peer-to-peer WiFi when there's no network. Your own music files play without internet; Apple Music and podcasts need a connection to stream or download.

🎵

Any Music Source

Import your own MP3s or search Apple Music. B2Gaudio works with the music you already have and love.

👥

Synchronized Listening

One person hosts a session, others join and hear the same music at the same time. Like a silent disco with your friends.

📋

Create Mixtapes

Build playlists from any source and share them with your group. Drag and drop to reorder, shuffle for variety.

🔀

Full Player Controls

Play, pause, skip, rewind, shuffle, and reorder your queue. All the controls you expect from a modern music player.

🔒

Private & Secure

Your music stays on your network. No data sent to servers, no tracking, no accounts required. Just you and your friends.

How It Works

1

Import or Connect Your Music

Add local audio files from your device or connect Apple Music. All your music in one place.

2

Host a Listening Session

Tap "Start Session" to create a room. Your phone becomes the host, broadcasting to anyone on the same WiFi network.

3

Friends Join In

Nearby friends open B2Gaudio and see your session. One tap to join, and they're listening along in sync.

4

Enjoy Together

Build the queue together, share mixtapes, and enjoy the music. It's that simple.
