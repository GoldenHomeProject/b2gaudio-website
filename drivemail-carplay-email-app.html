The best email app for CarPlay

Apple CarPlay ships with maps, music, calls, and messages — but no email. Here's how to actually handle your inbox while you drive.

Short answer: Apple's Mail app isn't available on CarPlay, and there's no built-in way to read or answer email from the car. The practical option is a dedicated hands-free email app. DriveMail Voice reads your unread email aloud — sender, subject, and body — and lets you reply or forward by voice, right from the CarPlay screen.

Download DriveMail Voice — free

Why CarPlay has no email app

CarPlay only shows apps in the categories Apple approves for the car, and mail was never one of them. That's why you can navigate and play a podcast but can't hear the email that just arrived — unless you add an app built specifically for it.

What to look for

Reads the full message

Not just the notification — sender, subject, and body, in order.

Voice replies

Answer and forward by speaking, without touching your phone.

Your providers

Gmail, Outlook, and iCloud in one merged unread stream.

Privacy

On-device processing and no backend servers. Cached mail is encrypted on your phone.

DriveMail Voice checks every box

It's a modern, CarPlay-native email client: AI summaries for long messages, adjustable reading speed, skip and replay controls, and smart filtering that drops signatures and legal disclaimers so you only hear what matters.
