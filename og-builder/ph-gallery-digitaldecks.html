Scan

Point at the number
in the corner.

The phone reads the collector number (like 4/102) on the device. No photo is saved or uploaded. Then it shows the set, the rarity in plain words (“about 1 in 13 packs”) and a rough price for your printing (regular, holo or reverse holo).

Prices are rough asking-price estimates from public listings, not appraisals. Please don't use them to decide what to pay for, sell or insure a card.
