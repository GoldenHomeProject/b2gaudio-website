Why it stays in sync

It isn't Bluetooth.

Doesn't need
  • ✕ Bluetooth
  • ✕ An account
  • ✕ A cable or dongle
  • ✕ A speaker
  • ✕ A WiFi router
Just needs
  • ✓ Two or more iPhones
  • ✓ The same WiFi, or none

Bluetooth's latency is unpredictable and unfixable from an app. This is a direct WiFi connection, which is exactly why it holds together.

Under the hood

Phones agreeing on what time it is.

1

Measure the clock gap

An NTP-style handshake on join — ping, pong, reject the round-trip outliers, take the median.

2

Schedule, don't shout

The host never says "play now." It says "start this track at instant T," and every phone converts T into its own clock.

3

Correct without clicks

Drift on local files is trimmed by nudging playback rate a fraction of a percent — inaudible — instead of seeking. A test fails the build if any correction floor regresses past 50 ms.
