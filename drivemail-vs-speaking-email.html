DriveMail Voice vs Speaking Email

There are really only two email apps for CarPlay. Here's how they compare.

The gist: both apps read your email aloud in the car. DriveMail Voice is the newer option, built for modern iOS, with voice replies and forwards, AI summaries of long emails, adjustable reading speed, and Gmail, Outlook, and iCloud in one merged stream — with all processing on-device.
