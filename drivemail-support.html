Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my email account?

Open DriveMail Voice and tap "Add Account" on the home screen. Choose Gmail, Outlook, or iCloud and follow the sign-in prompts. For Outlook, you'll sign in through Microsoft's official login page. For Gmail, you'll create an app password in your Google Account security settings and enter it in the app. For iCloud, you'll need an app-specific password from your Apple ID settings.

Does DriveMail Voice work with CarPlay?

Yes! DriveMail Voice is fully compatible with Apple CarPlay. When you connect to CarPlay, the app automatically starts reading your unread emails. You can pause, skip, reply, and mark as read directly from your car's display.

Is my email data stored on a server?

No. DriveMail Voice processes everything on your device. Email is fetched straight from your provider to your phone and never sent to any server of ours. Unread mail is cached on your device, encrypted at rest, and cleared when you remove the account or tap Clear cache. Your credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.

How do I reply to an email by voice?

After an email is read aloud, say "Reply" or tap the Reply button. Dictate your response using your voice. The app will read back your message and ask you to confirm before sending. You can re-record if it didn't capture your words correctly.

What is the AI Summary feature?

AI Summary generates a short summary of long emails so you can quickly understand the key points. All summarization is done on-device — no email content is ever sent to external AI services.

How do I remove ads?

Tap the "Remove Ads" button on the Settings screen to make a one-time purchase of $0.99 through the App Store. This permanently removes the banner ad from the home screen. You can restore your purchase on any device using the "Restore Purchases" option.

Why doesn't the app read certain parts of my email?

DriveMail Voice intelligently skips signatures, legal disclaimers, confidentiality notices, tracking links, and other boilerplate content so you only hear the parts that matter. The full email is always visible on screen if you want to read it yourself.

Can I adjust the reading speed?

Yes! Use the Speed controls on the playback screen to switch between Slow, Normal, and Fast reading speeds. The change takes effect immediately, even mid-sentence.

How do I disconnect an email account?

Go to Settings in the app and tap the account you want to remove. You can also revoke access directly through your email provider's security settings (Google Account, Microsoft Account, or Apple ID).

The app isn't reading my emails. What should I do?

Make sure you have an active internet connection and that your email account is properly connected. Try removing and re-adding the account. If the issue persists, please contact us at b2gaudio@gmail.com with details about your device and email provider.
